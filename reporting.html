Reproducible pipeline

Method, provenance & limitations

Measured, joined and retained without fabricating missing traffic or administrative coverage.

01

Repair coordinates

Routes are reinterpreted in EPSG:32636, forced to two dimensions and transformed to EPSG:4326 for browser mapping.

02

Assign stable IDs

Each link ID hashes its district, source code, road name and geometry; exact duplicates receive deterministic occurrence suffixes.

03

Intersect villages

Every line is intersected with 42,816 supplied village polygons. Boundary overlaps are proportionally scaled so a link cannot be double-counted.

04

Join without invention

AADT, PCU and speed are populated only from exact source-code matches. Missing traffic remains null and is measured as a coverage gap.
